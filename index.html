Dispute & Bureau Playbook

Step-by-step tactical frameworks for executing FCRA disputes, demanding debt validation, and scrubbing inaccurate tradelines.

1. The 30-Day Statutory Rule

Under Section 611 of the Fair Credit Reporting Act (FCRA), credit bureaus have exactly 30 days from the date of receipt to investigate your dispute. If they fail to verify or respond within 30 days, federal law mandates immediate deletion.

2. Direct Furnisher Disputes (Section 623)

Don't just dispute with Equifax or Experian. Send a certified dispute letter directly to the original bank, hospital, or collection agency. Furnishers are legally barred from verifying inaccurate data.

3. Certified Mail Protocol

Always send legal correspondence via USPS Certified Mail with Return Receipt Requested. The green card signature provides indisputable legal proof of delivery.
